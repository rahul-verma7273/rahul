About Balrampur District

Balrampur - The Northern Gem of Uttar Pradesh

Balrampur district is located in the northern part of Uttar Pradesh, sharing its border with Nepal. It was carved out of Gonda district on May 22, 1997. The district is known for its rich cultural heritage and agricultural significance.

The district headquarters is located in Balrampur town, which serves as the administrative center. The region is primarily agrarian, with the majority of the population engaged in farming and related activities.

Balrampur has several historical and religious sites that attract visitors from across the state. The district is also known for its wildlife sanctuary, which is home to diverse flora and fauna.

Key Highlights:

  • Established: 22 May 1997 (carved from Gonda district)
  • Total Area: 3,349 square kilometers
  • Population: Approximately 2.1 million (2011 census)
  • Literacy Rate: 49.51% (as per 2011 census)
  • Main Rivers: Rapti, Burhi Rapti, Kuano
  • Major Crops: Rice, Wheat, Sugarcane, Pulses
  • Famous For: Balrampur Tiger Reserve, Devipatan Temple
